## RDP Nexus 1.0.6

**Install:** download **`RDPNexus-setup.exe`** below and double-click it. It now installs with no
questions and starts RDP Nexus (per-user, no admin rights; a running older version is closed and
replaced automatically; your settings are kept).

### Fixed
- **App switching in background mode did nothing** (1.0.5). Tested live: the Remote Desktop client
  ignores keys sent to it while its window is not the active window. Background mode now works like
  this: when an app switch is due, at your first 3-second pause in typing and mouse use the RDP
  window comes forward for a moment (restored if minimized), the same real Alt+Tab / Page Down key
  presses that work when the RDP window is in front are sent, and the window you were using gets focus
  back straight away (the RDP window is minimized again if it was).
- **Test now** works again with a full-screen session: press it and keep still; the RDP window comes
  forward for a moment and RDP Nexus gets focus back.
- **A due switch no longer waits for a long idle period or restarts the whole countdown** when you
  are busy, in the background or with the RDP window in front: it happens at your first 3-second
  pause in typing and mouse use (the countdown shows **due** until then).
- **One double-click install:** the installer no longer shows wizard pages or asks you to close
  RDP Nexus first. "Launch at sign-in" is switched on or off in the app (**Options → Startup**).
- Each background switch now keeps the RDP window in front for well under a second: it scrolls the
  app that is showing, then switches, without waiting in between.
- The RDP client injects a couple of key events when it gets focus; they are no longer mistaken for
  you typing (which cancelled the switch).

### Details
- If you type or click during that moment, the keys stop at once (any held key is released) and the
  switch is retried at your next pause. While you keep working it waits for a 3-second pause, checking
  every second; the countdown shows **due** meanwhile and the full countdown is never restarted.
- If Windows refuses to let RDP Nexus bring the window forward, it says so in the Log and tries again
  a minute later.
- A session on another virtual desktop is never brought forward (that would switch your desktop).
- Keys are never sent any other way; only the remote-pointer nudges are posted messages (best effort).
- Turn off **Options → Background** to act only while the RDP window is active, as in 1.0.4.
