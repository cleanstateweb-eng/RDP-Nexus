## RDP Nexus 1.0.8

**Install:** download **`RDPNexus-setup.exe`** below and double-click it: it installs with no questions,
closes and replaces a running older version, keeps your settings and starts RDP Nexus.

### Fixed
- **Hiding the settings now shrinks the window** to just the header bar (it used to hide the settings
  and leave an empty window at full height); showing them grows it again.
- **The show/hide-settings button always has an arrow icon.** It is drawn by the app instead of taken
  from a Windows icon font.
- **No empty scrollbar strip in the settings tabs.** A tab only shows its scrollbar when its content
  is taller than the panel (mouse-wheel scrolling still works then).
