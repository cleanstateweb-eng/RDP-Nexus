## RDP Nexus 1.0.7

**Install:** download **`RDPNexus-setup.exe`** below and double-click it: it installs with no questions,
closes and replaces a running older version, keeps your settings and starts RDP Nexus.

### Fixed
- **Settings were cut off with no way to scroll.** On the **Keys** tab the *Every (min)* row was
  clipped and **Test now** could not be reached. The settings area is now taller so every tab fits,
  and each tab scrolls with the mouse wheel (with a thin scrollbar) if its content is ever taller
  than the panel.

Everything from 1.0.6 is included: background app switching that brings the RDP window forward for
under a second at your first 3-second pause, and the one double-click installer.
